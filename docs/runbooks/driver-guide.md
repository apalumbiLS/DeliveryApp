# Driver Guide

Accepting jobs, buying materials, and getting paid.

> This replaces the Slack thread process in `External Notes/Low Tech Flow.pdf`. Slack is no longer used.

---

## What you need

- A phone with a browser. The driver app is a web app — **nothing to install**.
- Your login (email and password). The dispatcher sets this up and gives you the initial password.
- A way to take photos — the app uses your phone's camera.

Add the app to your home screen for quick access.

---

## Getting a job

The dispatcher sends you a **link** to the job (by text or however they reach you — the app sends nothing itself yet). Open jobs also show under **Open jobs** on your **Jobs** page. Push notifications for new jobs are planned.

Each job shows you:

| Shown before you accept           | Shown after you accept                   |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Your payout                       | The on-site contact's name and phone     |
| The store and its address         | The drop-off instructions                |
| The full delivery address         | The status steps                         |
| The items and quantities          |                                          |
| The vehicle needed (Car or Truck) |                                          |

### Accepting

Tap **Accept job**. The job is yours, and it moves to **In progress** on your Jobs page.

**It is first-come, first-served.** If two drivers tap at the same moment, exactly one wins and the other is told immediately that it's taken. You will never be assigned a job someone else already has, and you will never drive to a store for a job that isn't yours.

### If you can't take it

Just don't accept it. There is no **Decline** button yet (planned), so the dispatcher can't see that you passed. If they called you about it, tell them.

## Doing the job

### 1. Go to the store

The job page shows the store address. Tap **Navigate** to open it in your maps app (Apple Maps on iPhone, your default maps app on Android). When you arrive, tap **I'm at the store**.

> **Check the store carefully.** The store was selected by the dispatcher, not by the system, and the cart email does not tell us which store the customer used. If something seems wrong — the items aren't sold here, the store is far from the delivery address — call the dispatcher _before_ you buy anything.

### 2. Buy the items

Buy exactly what the job card lists. The customer's cart is a **cart, not an order** — they never purchased it. You are making the purchase.

Pay with the company card or method the dispatcher told you about.

**Keep the receipt.** You will photograph it.

### 3. Photograph the receipt

After loading, take a photo of the receipt and tap **Items purchased**. The photo goes to the dispatcher. This is the evidence behind the charge.

You can tap **Skip the photo**, but you must give a reason, and the dispatcher will see it. Don't make a habit of it.

### 4. Head out

Tap **On my way**. The customer sees this in their portal; no email or text is sent. Tap **Navigate** for directions to the site.

### 5. Deliver

At the site, follow the drop-off instructions on the job card. If no one is there and the instructions say drop-off, leave the materials where instructed.

Photograph the materials where you left them and tap **Delivered**. The customer sees this photo; it is their proof the materials arrived. Skipping it requires a reason. Delivered is the last step.

> If a handoff is required and nobody is available, call the dispatcher. Do not leave materials if the instructions say to hand off.

## Status buttons, in order

| Button               | When                                              |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **I'm at the store** | You've arrived                                    |
| **Items purchased**  | After buying and loading — photograph the receipt |
| **On my way**        | You're driving to the site                        |
| **Delivered**        | Materials are delivered — photograph the drop-off |

You can only move forward; there is no undo. If you tap the wrong one, tap **Call dispatch** on the job page. The dispatcher can't step a job back, but can take it back from you (**Release driver**) and post it again.

---

## Getting paid

The customer pays the platform, not you. Your payout is set per job; the app does not send payouts yet.

The job page shows your payout before you accept, and it can't change once you accept, so there are no surprises.

---

## Quick reference

**Before you accept:** check the store and the item count. If the store looks wrong, don't accept.

**Before you buy:** confirm the items are actually at that store. The cart is a request, not a reservation — an item may be out of stock. If something is out of stock, call the on-site contact before substituting anything. Can't reach them? Call dispatch.

**Before you leave the store:** photograph the receipt.

**Before you leave the site:** photograph the delivery.

**If anything goes wrong:** tap **Call dispatch** (864-346-8066). The app has no way to report a problem mid-job yet, and a phone call is faster than a wrong delivery.
